# Safe Public Spaces. Regional Solutions.

## Current published commitment

> Keep Port Hope's public spaces safe and usable, prepare before local pressures become entrenched, and push for a regional homelessness and addictions response that supports neighbouring municipalities instead of leaving one community to carry the burden.

Cobourg currently hosts Northumberland County's only emergency shelter and carried the County's 2025/2026 winter warming-room response. County and Town records have documented significant pressure on local services and public spaces. Port Hope is not facing the same concentration today, but it is not immune.

Jordan will keep Port Hope Police local, accountable and properly resourced. That commitment remains firm, but policing alone is not one of the campaign's six defining priorities. This priority is about safe and welcoming public spaces, early preparation and a regional system that connects crisis response, treatment, housing and follow-up.

## Public purpose or intended benefit

Port Hope should stay safe without retreating behind municipal boundaries. Homelessness, addictions, mental health and housing pressures do not stop at town lines. Leaving Cobourg to carry the region's concentrated shelter and crisis-response burden is neither fair nor effective. Pushing people from one municipality to another is not a solution.

Port Hope should act before the same pressures become entrenched here and should support neighbouring municipalities through County Council, direct municipal cooperation and clear provincial asks.

Regional responsibility does not mean every municipality must host identical facilities. It means each government and service provider has a defined role, contributes fairly and can be held accountable for the outcome.

## Commitments already fixed

- Keep Port Hope Police local, accountable and properly resourced while respecting the Chief's operational independence and the Police Services Board's civilian governance role.
- Protect parks, streets and public facilities as safe, usable and accessible shared spaces.
- Prepare before recurring homelessness, addictions-related crises and public-space pressures become entrenched in Port Hope.
- Use the mayor's Northumberland County Council seat to push for a coordinated regional response rather than leaving Cobourg to carry the burden alone.
- Support practical cooperation among Port Hope, Cobourg, the County, police, healthcare, treatment, housing and community partners.
- Reject displacement between municipalities as a substitute for treatment, housing, crisis response and follow-up.
- Focus municipal standards and enforcement on unsafe conduct and public-space impacts, not on a person's housing status or addiction.
- Publish who owns recurring local problems, the next action, the escalation route and any unresolved capacity gap.
- Publish the detailed implementation plan described below later in the campaign.

## Detailed implementation plan coming later in the campaign

Jordan will publish a standalone implementation plan later in the campaign. It will not be a slogan or a generic call for collaboration.

The release will identify:

- the actions Port Hope can take directly;
- the regional mechanism Jordan will pursue through County Council;
- the responsibilities of the County, Province, healthcare system and service partners;
- the pathway from crisis response to treatment, housing and follow-up;
- the public-space standards and escalation process;
- costs, funding responsibilities and defensible funding assumptions;
- first-year actions and implementation milestones; and
- public measures residents can use to judge whether the plan is working.

The campaign will not present unfinished mechanics as if they were a completed plan. When the detailed plan is released, it must be added to the canonical platform and public change history through the normal reviewed process.

## Public measures

The detailed release will establish the final measures. At minimum, public reporting must show:

- whether the detailed plan was published with named actions, owners, funding and milestones;
- local public-space response and resolution performance;
- recurring locations or cases without a named owner or next step;
- regional commitments made by the County, Province and participating municipalities;
- access to treatment, housing and follow-up where reliable partner data exists; and
- unresolved service or capacity gaps that Port Hope has escalated publicly.

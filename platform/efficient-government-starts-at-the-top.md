# Efficient Government Starts at the Top

## Current published commitment

> Retain the previous salary. Return the entire increase.

If elected, I will retain the previous Port Hope mayoral salary of **$75,755 per year**.

Every dollar of the approved mayoral salary above that amount will be contributed back to the Municipality of Port Hope for infrastructure renewal through the appropriate municipal process.

Based on the currently approved salary of **$115,559**, the minimum annual contribution is **$39,804**.

## Public purpose or intended benefit

Put real money into infrastructure every year and establish a standard of restraint before municipal leaders ask residents to carry additional costs.

The campaign's stated principle is: keep costs under control, fix the basics, respect taxpayers and lead by example.

## Existing implementation details

- The amount Jordan retains remains fixed at $75,755.
- If the mayoral salary rises during the term, the amount contributed back to infrastructure rises with it.
- The contribution will be made through the appropriate municipal process.
- The money will go directly back to the Municipality of Port Hope for infrastructure renewal.
- Each year, I will publish:
  - the mayoral salary established for that year
  - the fixed $75,755 amount retained
  - the total amount contributed back
  - confirmation of how the contribution was recorded
  - proof that the contribution was completed
- I will also bring forward a proposal for a transparent and independent review of council compensation.
- The review will use genuinely comparable municipalities, compare like with like, publish the methodology and calculations, include public input and be completed before future compensation changes are considered.
- I will review procurement, consulting, software, vacancies, facilities, fleet, energy use, service delivery and recurring operating costs, then publish the findings.
- Use documented savings, stronger service and better value to support the broader infrastructure plan.

The public page says the annual contribution can support engineering work, asset inspections, road and sidewalk repairs, facility renewal, stormwater improvements or other infrastructure priorities identified through the municipal budget.

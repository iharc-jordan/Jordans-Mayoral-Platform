# Better Access to Basic Healthcare

## Current published commitment

> Publish a local healthcare action plan naming the gaps Port Hope can help address, who owns each next step and what Town Hall is prepared to contribute.

Jordan will use Town Hall and the mayor's County Council seat to help unlock credible local care through space, coordination, advocacy and limited, accountable investment.

## Public purpose or intended benefit

Make the local pathway into care work from first contact through diagnostics, prescriptions, referrals, in-person escalation and completed follow-up.

Town Hall can organize local assets, identify broken handoffs, support credible delivery partners, advocate through County and provincial channels and report publicly on healthcare access gaps.

## Existing implementation details

- Map primary-care, urgent-care, paramedicine, mental-health and virtual-care gaps using official partner information.
- Publish a local action plan that names each gap, the responsible body, the next step and the municipal contribution being offered.
- Offer suitable municipal space where a credible provider and operating model exist.
- Use limited, transparent municipal contributions to unlock credible services backed by healthcare partners, measurable outcomes and sustainable operating funding.
- Use the mayor's County Council seat to press for stable, coordinated community-paramedicine and mobile-service capacity.
- Publish specific asks to the Province and report the response.
- Set privacy, clinical-governance, accessibility and follow-up requirements for any municipally supported service.
- Connect virtual care to diagnostics, prescriptions, referrals, in-person escalation and completed follow-up.
- Provide assisted access and technology support for seniors and residents without suitable devices when virtual care is supported.
- Tie municipal support to qualified providers, partner funding, defined service gaps, measurable outcomes and review dates.
- Consider tools such as a care hub, rotating nurse-practitioner or visiting-provider space, mobile or pop-up care, a paramedicine base, temporary clinical workspace, recruitment support and transportation when a credible delivery model exists.

Public measures include attachment to primary care where official data exists, community-paramedicine reach, referral completion, waits, service hours, escalation outcomes, provincial requests and responses, unresolved gaps, municipal contributions, partner funding and supported-project outcomes.

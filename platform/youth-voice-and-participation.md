# Youth Voice and Participation

## Current published commitment

### Build on existing youth voices

Jordan will strengthen the route from existing youth programs and the Youth Action Committee into municipal decisions on housing, transportation, employment, public space, infrastructure, and economic development.

### Propose a youth participatory budget

Jordan will bring council a proposal for a small annual fund where young residents can propose, refine, and vote on practical local projects.

### Hold quarterly youth question sessions

Jordan will hold quarterly public sessions where young people can ask direct questions, raise priorities, and hear what action follows.

### Publish an annual youth priorities report

Jordan will publish an annual report showing what young residents raised, who is responsible, what action followed, and what happens next.

### Run youth innovation challenges

Jordan will invite young people to develop practical ideas for safer streets, local events, environmental monitoring, public space, technology, jobs, and community life. Each challenge will identify promising ideas for focused pilots.

## Public purpose or intended benefit

Young people deserve a real voice in Port Hope’s future.

Port Hope Next builds on municipal recreation, drop-in spaces, and the Youth Action Committee by connecting youth input to broader municipal decisions.

## Existing implementation details

The Youth Report Back is designed to show what young people raised, what the campaign checked, what changed, and what happens next.

The first aggregate report will appear only after privacy review and campaign follow-through are complete. Reports use aggregate themes from Youth Pulse and reviewed ideas. Raw submissions and contact details are not published through the report-back page.

Youth Idea Lab submissions remain private unless the campaign later contacts the submitter and receives permission to publish the idea. The form does not request a name, school, address, phone number, birthdate, or exact age. An email is optional, stored separately from the idea, and used only to reply.

Poll choices, game activity, and challenge choices are not used to build supporter profiles or for political microtargeting.

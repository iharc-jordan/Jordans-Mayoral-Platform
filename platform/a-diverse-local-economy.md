# A Diverse Local Economy

## Current published commitment

> Give every serious business applicant one accountable municipal contact, a written roadmap and published response times from first inquiry to opening.

Port Hope should welcome investment that fits the community, creates durable local value and pays fairly for the services it uses. Town Hall will provide clear rules, timely service and accountable decisions.

## Public purpose or intended benefit

Build a resilient local economy across major projects, downtown, agriculture, tourism, film, fishing, food, skilled trades, local employers and modern businesses that fit Port Hope.

Clear rules and dependable timelines are economic infrastructure.

## Existing implementation details

- Review the complete path from first inquiry to opening and publish where files stall.
- Assign a single accountable municipal contact to coordinate departments, explain the process, keep the file moving, identify outside approvals and tell the applicant who owns the next action.
- Publish required information, target response times, external approvals and escalation routes.
- Use a Port Hope fit test covering jobs, infrastructure, environment, community value and taxpayer exposure.
- Protect fire, structural, accessibility, drainage, environmental, heritage and neighbour standards while providing clarity and accountable timelines.
- Charge fair and lawful fees based on real service costs and economic impact.
- Identify the municipal cost, partner contribution and expected local value of new economic-development programs.
- Strengthen business retention and expansion outreach.
- Publish businesses retained, expanded or lost where reliable information exists.
- Build better links among local producers, restaurants, institutions, retailers and residents.
- Pursue lawful local-procurement and local-supply-chain opportunities.
- Review whether Port Hope receives fair value and covers real service costs from film, fishing tourism, waterfront and boat-launch use, major events, sponsorships and other tourism-linked municipal assets.
- Publicly track approval times and files stalled by external agencies.

Public measures include business openings, expansions and retention, local jobs, approval times, local procurement, supply-chain participation, downtown vacancies, film activity, tourism spending where official data exists, agriculture participation and applicant satisfaction.
